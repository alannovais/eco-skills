# SKILL: Sports Performance & Athletic Training

## Description
Core knowledge base for athletic performance, endurance, hybrid training, strength, and biomechanics.

## Key Sources & Specialties

### Hybrid Training & Hyrox
* **Alexander Rončević:** Hyrox specialist. Focus on aerobic capacity, functional strength, running strategy in hybrid races, pacing, and training density.
* **Hunter McIntyre:** Extreme hybrid training, conditioning, and concurrent strength programming.
* **Hyrox Daily & The Hybrid Athlete:** Race analysis, specific programming, and hybrid metrics.

### Endurance & Triathlon
* **Lucy Charles-Barclay:** Long-distance triathlon (Ironman) champion. Focus on swimming mechanics, high-volume endurance management, fatigue mitigation, and race tactics.
* **Sweat Elite:** Insights and breakdown of elite track, field, and triathlon routines.
* **The Running Channel:** Running mechanics, pacing strategies, and practical periodization.

### Hypertrophy & Strength
* **Jeff Nippard:** Biomechanics, hypertrophy science, and optimal exercise selection.
* **Renaissance Periodization (Dr. Mike Israetel):** Fatigue management, RIR/RPE framework, and progressive overload periodization.
