# SKILL: Health, Physiology & Evidence-Based Nutrition

## Description
Scientific reference framework for sports medicine, metabolic health, recovery, and evidence-based nutrition.

## Key Sources & Evidence Base

### Physiology, Sports Medicine & Injury
* **Barbell Medicine (Dr. Jordan Feigenbaum & Dr. Austin Baraki):** Evidence-based clinical diagnosis applied to strength training, pain science, and injury rehabilitation.
* **Huberman Lab (Dr. Andrew Huberman):** Neurobiology, sleep hygiene, hormonal optimization, and recovery protocols.

### Sports Nutrition & Metabolism
* **Biolayne (Dr. Layne Norton, PhD):** Energy balance, protein synthesis, body composition, and debunking diet myths.
* **The Performance Nutrition Podcast (Dr. Marc Bubbs):** Carbohydrate periodization, ergogenic aids, and endurance nutrition.
* **We Do Science / IOPN Podcast (Dr. Laurent Bannock):** Advanced sports nutrition research and metabolic physiology.
* **Greg Doucette:** Caloric density strategies and dietary adherence.

### Scientific Research Analysis
* **Stronger by Science (Greg Nuckols & Eric Trexler):** Methodological analysis of peer-reviewed literature on strength and metabolism.
